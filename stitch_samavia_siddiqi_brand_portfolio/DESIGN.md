## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elevated, warm editorial presence for Samavia Siddiqi’s personal portfolio. It marries modern Scandinavian precision with high-fashion editorial warmth, targeting design leaders, founders, and cultural collaborators. The aesthetic avoids cold tech tropes in favor of tactile confidence: organic off-whites, terracotta punctuation, and structured charcoal typography.

Key emotional attributes:
- **Discerning & Thoughtful:** Unhurried negative space, intentional asymmetrical balances, and rigorous typography.
- **Architectural & Tactile:** Precise geometric chevron motifs cutting cleanly through soft, linen-like surfaces.
- **Warm Authority:** Confident, deep charcoal text resting on warm, sun-softened cream canvases rather than harsh bleached whites.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Micro-Details**: airy composition punctuated by hairline vector geometry, subtle dot-grid anchors, and understated terracotta ambient glows.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, editorial canvas grounded in deep earth pigments and warm light.

- **Primary (`#E3A87C` - Soft Terracotta):** Used selectively for key interactive focal points, selected states, and brand framing accents. Never over-saturated; it behaves as an architectural accent rather than a loud warning hue.
- **Secondary / Contrast (`#2B2420` - Deep Warm Charcoal):** The anchor for high-contrast typography, structural rules, and primary text. Its warm undertone harmonizes with the cream canvas while maintaining AAA legibility.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7EFE6` - Warm Cream):** The foundational backdrop. It rejects synthetic paper white to create an organic, gallery-like reading experience.
- **Surface Alt (`#EFE5D8` - Tinted Sand):** Used for nested cards, secondary sections, and structural container differentiation.
- **Translucent Accents:**
  - Border Accents / Hairlines: `#2B24201A` (10% charcoal) for default structural divisions; `#E3A87C40` (25% terracotta) for active or featured borders.
  - Dot Grid / Subtle Patterns: `#E3A87C2E` (18% terracotta) placed in canvas corners.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the sculptural, avant-garde character of **Syne** against the neutral, crystalline legibility of **Inter**.

- **Syne (Headings & Labels):** Applied with deliberate negative letter-spacing in display scales to produce an authoritative, magazine-cover presence. In uppercase labels, tracked out between `+0.08em` and `+0.12em` to create crisp navigational metadata.
- **Inter (Body & Captions):** Provides an objective, unpretentious reading rhythm across case studies and editorial copy. Text line heights are kept generous (`1.6`–`1.65`) to maintain optical ease against warm backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an **asymmetric 12-column editorial grid** that permits intentional shifts in column spans (e.g., pairing a 5-column project narrative with a 7-column media showcase).

- **Grid Mechanics:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `2.5rem` gutters, `4rem` outer margin. Max content container width: `1360px`.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2.5rem` outer margin.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer margin.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Headings routinely break out by 1 column offset compared to body content below, creating optical tension and editorial pacing.
- **Section Breaks:** Spaced at generous rhythms (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) punctuated by hairline dividers and chevron vectors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal layering and warm micro-diffusions** rather than dark dropshadows.

- **Base to Container:** Elements sit flush on the `#F7EFE6` canvas or lift slightly on `#EFE5D8` surfaces with a 1px boundary of `#2B242014`.
- **Card Hover & Focus State:** When hovering over interactive portfolio cards, the element transitions up `-4px` along the Y-axis, supported by an ambient terracotta glow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(43, 36, 32, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px #E3A87C66;`
- **Overlays & Modals:** Modals use `#F7EFE6` with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) tinted by `rgba(43, 36, 32, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced geometry: crisp hairline framing mixed with intentional, tactile roundness.

- **Curvature:** Component containers, media frames, and cards use an explicit `12px` (equivalent to level `2` token architecture) corner radius. This softens the heavy geometric impact of Syne without drifting into casual bubble-like forms.
- **Interactive Badges / Pills:** Small status tags and metadata chips utilize fully pill-shaped profiles (`9999px`) to create optical distinction against rectangular media tiles.
- **Vector Chevron Accents:** Thin, dual-line chevron vectors (`#2B2420` paired adjacent with `#E3A87C`) strictly set at 45°/135° angles serve as corner anchors, scroll indicators, and section-break cut-throughs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#E3A87C`, label `#2B2420` in `Syne` semi-bold, uppercase tracking. Radius `12px`. Hover shifts tone slightly deeper with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(227, 168, 124, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px border `#2B242040`, text `#2B2420`. On hover: border color transitions to `#2B2420`, background shifts to `#2B242008`.
- **Text Link:** Underlined with an offset 1px stroke in `#E3A87C`; transitions to `#2B2420` on hover with an arrow glyph moving 3px right.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Container:** Background `#EFE5D8`, radius `12px`, border `1px solid #2B242014`.
- **Hover Behavior:** Translates `-4px` vertically, border highlights to `#E3A87C`, applying warm ambient diffusion.
- **Header & Meta:** Category labels set in `label-sm` uppercase Syne, colored `#2B242080`.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, padding `0 12px`.
- Background `#F7EFE6`, border `1px solid #2B242020`, text `#2B2420` in `label-sm`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base:** Background `#F7EFE6`, border `1px solid #2B242026`, radius `12px`, padding `14px 18px`.
- **Focus:** Border shifts to `#E3A87C`, outline ring `2px solid #E3A87C33`. Placeholder text `#2B242066`.

### Visual Details & Dividers
- **Section Dividers:** 1px rule `#2B24201A` interrupted centrally or offset to the right by an interlocking two-stroke chevron (`1px #2B2420` + `1px #E3A87C`).
- **Dot-Grid Elements:** Placed strictly in fixed 4x4 or 6x6 node clusters (`3px` diameter dots on `16px` centers) in the Hero top-right and Contact bottom-left margins using `#E3A87C40`.
